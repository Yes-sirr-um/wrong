Prologue

Before the lights go down

Every evening starts somewhere quieter than it ends. Maybe it's a walk along the water as the sun drops, the sky going from gold to plum, the air cooling just enough to make you want to stay close.

Maybe it's a look across the room that says more than either of you planned to. Nothing has to happen yet. The night is still ahead, and it is patient.

A night in, written for two

The Way We Rise and Fall

Some nights don't stay steady. They climb. They drop. They soak through everything.

Begin
slow, then closer

I.

The first slow touch

Your skin is already warm when my hands find you. I trace a path slowly, from your shoulder down to the small of your back, until the air between us seems to change its shape.

You breathe in and hold it, and I feel your heartbeat quicken under my palm. Neither of us pretends this isn't what we wanted all evening.

II.

Playful, then closer

I tease a little, light and unhurried. A laugh slips out of you, then a softer sound, and I love how easily you melt when I'm this close.

Your fingers tangle in my hair. I pull back just enough to make you reach for me, then draw you in again, slower this time, until the space between us disappears.

Outside, the city hums low and far away. Inside, there is only the soft light, the rustle of sheets, and the easy laughter that comes when two people stop pretending they're not already in the middle of something good.

III.

Holding on through the high

There's a moment when everything tightens, then loosens all at once, and we are both a little unsteady. I stay with you through all of it, your breath against my neck, the shaky laugh that follows.

Afterward the room is quiet again. I keep my arms around you and trace slow circles on your back, just to feel you settle.

We don't rush to get up. We talk about nothing in particular: the streetlight outside, the book on the nightstand, the way the sheets have twisted around our legs.

Eventually the window fades from black to blue, and we realize we've been awake all night. Neither of us minds. Some nights are measured in sleep, and some are measured in the things we said to each other before it came.

Up. Down. Warm. Playful. Completely gone for each other.

This is the kind of night I want with you, the ones that rise and fall until neither of us can tell where the closeness begins.
